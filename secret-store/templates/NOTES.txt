The store is ready once its status says Valid. While it is not, every secret
that depends on it stays empty: the certificate of a gateway listener does not
appear and the listener comes up without TLS.

The usual reasons are the role missing in StarVault, the role not bound to this
namespace and ServiceAccount, or a certificate authority StarVault presents that
the cluster does not trust (store.caBundle).

Other releases point at this store by its name, for example an ingress-gateway
certificate takes it in the "Хранилище" field.
